## Hi, I'm Kanan Gabilov 👋

IT student in the **Baku Engineering University × INHA University** dual-degree program, currently on my senior exchange year in Incheon, South Korea.

I'm interested in **how people interact with technology**, and especially with AI. I design interfaces, test them with users, and prototype them so they can actually be used.

### 🔭 Focus
- **Human–computer interaction**: user research, usability, embodied and gesture-based interaction
- **Human–AI interaction**: how people and AI systems work together
- **Interaction and UX design**: from research to Figma prototype

### 📌 Selected work
- **[Air Drum](https://github.com/gabilov/air-drum)**: turns a phone into a drumstick using motion sensors, so playing is a gesture instead of a tap
- **Human–AI collaboration for astronauts**: abstract accepted at the **76th International Astronautical Congress (IAC 2025)**
- **Harmoniq**: team platform; I worked on the ML side and the frontend
- **SM Fintech**: fintech product design case study (Behance)
- **Folded Letters**: letter-writing app concept for foldable phones, where the outer screen is the envelope and the inner screen is the letter
